# D1 — Doctrine sources (annotated)

All web checks performed 2026-06-12. Rule: primary text cited where the number lives; web mirrors
listed as *verification*, not as authority. Conflicts recorded, not resolved by fiat.

## 1. Garden City — Howard 1898/1902

- **Howard, E. (1902 [1898]). *Garden Cities of To-morrow* (orig. *To-morrow: A Peaceful Path to Real
  Reform*).** Ch. 1-2 + Diagrams 2-3 carry every number used: 6,000-acre estate, 1,000-acre town,
  32,000 population (30,000 + 2,000 agricultural), Central Park 145 acres, Grand Avenue 420 ft / 115
  acres, six 120-ft boulevards, 5,500 lots of avg 20x130 ft, six wards of ~5,000. Chs. 2-4 carry the
  rate-rent / community-trust financial machinery used for the stakeholder prior.
- Verification: [Wikipedia: Garden city movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garden_city_movement);
  [PlanningTank: Garden City Movement](https://planningtank.com/planning-theory/garden-city-movement)
  — both reproduce the 6,000/1,000/32,000 and 120-420 ft figures. Confidence high.
- Note: residential fraction 0.33 is *derived* (5,500 x 2,600 sq ft), not stated by Howard.

## 2. Ville Radieuse / Athens Charter — Le Corbusier 1933/1935

- **Le Corbusier (1935). *La Ville radieuse*.** Density of the housing sector 1,000 inh/ha; à-redent
  slabs on pilotis; ground given to the pedestrian.
- **CIAM (1943 [1933]). *La Charte d'Athènes*.** Pts. 23-29 (high buildings set wide apart, dwelling
  zone primacy), 59-64 (circulation separation), 95 (private interest subordinated to collective).
- Verification: [ScienceDirect S2095263521000388](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2095263521000388)
  (urban characterisation of Corbusier's schemes; 1,000/ha vs 400-500/ha existing);
  [Wikipedia: Ville Radieuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ville_Radieuse) (24 cruciform towers,
  200 m, 60 storeys; ~1.5M demonstration population);
  [Grokipedia: Towers in the park](https://grokipedia.com/page/Towers_in_the_park) (coverage 5-15%);
  [Architecture Lab: Ville Radieuse](https://www.architecturelab.net/ville-radieuse-le-corbusier/) (95% open in tower district).
- **Conflict recorded:** business-district coverage ~5% is well attested; the housing-district 12%
  figure is the commonly quoted *La Ville radieuse* value but web sources mostly give the generic
  5-15% band → recorded as range [0.05, 0.12], confidence medium.

## 3. Neighbourhood Unit — Perry 1929

- **Perry, C. A. (1929). 'The Neighborhood Unit', Monograph I, *Regional Survey of New York and Its
  Environs*, vol. VII.** Six principles: size (one elementary school, 5,000-6,000 persons; worked
  example 160 acres @ 10 families/acre), boundaries (arterials on all sides), open space (~10%),
  institutions at centre, internal streets sized to load, shops at periphery junctions; 1/4-mile
  (400 m) school radius.
- Verification: [Wikipedia: Neighbourhood unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighbourhood_unit);
  [Grokipedia: Neighbourhood unit](https://grokipedia.com/page/Neighbourhood_unit);
  [PlanningTank](https://planningtank.com/planning-theory/clarence-a-perrys-neighborhood-unit);
  [Erickson & Highsmith, TC Record (PDF)](https://www.tc.columbia.edu/faculty/ate11/faculty-profile/files/EricksonHighsmith_TCRecord_OnlineFirst.pdf)
  — the latter documents the school-centred segregation use that feeds the developer/exclusion side
  of the stakeholder prior. Confidence high on all retained numbers.

## 4. Microrayon — SNiP-era Soviet norms

- **SNiP 2.07.01-89\* *Градостроительство. Планировка и застройка городских и сельских поселений*
  (Gosstroy USSR, 1989).** §2.11: microdistrict greenery >= 6 m²/person (3-5 m² in far-north
  subzones), excluding school/kindergarten sites — verified against full texts at
  [docs.cntd.ru/document/5200163](http://docs.cntd.ru/document/5200163) and
  [base.garant.ru/2305985](https://base.garant.ru/2305985/).
- Population 8,000-12,000; area 10-60 ha (<=80); 500-m limit for everyday public services; entrances
  <=300 m apart; through roads must not cross — via
  [Wikipedia: Microdistrict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microrayon) (citing SNiP 2.07.01-89) and
  [danarti.org microrayon essay](https://www.danarti.org/en/article/microrayon-where-half-of-urban--population-of-the-former-ussr-live/99).
- **Not re-verified against table text** (kept at medium confidence): kindergarten 300 m / school
  750 m radii (standard SNiP table values, widely cited in secondary literature); land-balance
  fractions of SNiP-era handbooks were NOT found verbatim → marked doctrine_silent rather than invented.
- Storey regime (5 → 9-16) is the standardized-series economy, not a SNiP number:
  [Springer: Large Housing Estates in the Baltic Countries](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-23392-1_4).

## 5. GB 50180-2018 — 城市居住区规划设计标准

- **GB 50180-2018 (MOHURD; effective 2018-12-01).** Full text consulted at
  [awhouse.art/gb50180-2018](https://www.awhouse.art/gb50180-2018) and
  [fqghj.net mirror](https://www.fqghj.net/show.aspx?tid=163&nid=3138).
  - Table 3.0.4: 15-min circle 50,000-100,000 (17k-32k units); 10-min 15,000-25,000; 5-min
    5,000-12,000; 居住街坊 1,000-3,000 persons / 300-1,000 units / 2-4 ha.
  - Table 4.0.2 (climate III/IV/V; Shanghai = III): FAR 1.0-1.2 / 1.3-1.6 / 1.7-2.1 / 2.2-2.8 /
    2.9-3.1; max building density 43/32/30/22/22%; min green rate 25/30/30/35/35%; max height
    18/27/36/54/80 m for the five height classes.
  - Table 4.0.4: per-capita public green 2.0 / 1.0 / 1.0 m²/person (15/10/5-min) with min park sizes
    5.0 / 1.0 / 0.4 ha; §4.0.7: block concentrated green >= 0.5 m²/person (new), 0.35 (renewal).
  - §6.0.2: road network density >= 8 km/km², road spacing <= 300 m, preferred 150-250 m.
- **Conflict recorded and resolved:** one mirror extraction (fqghj) returned 1.5/1.0/0.5 m²/person
  for Table 4.0.4; the verbatim full-text quote from awhouse gives 2.0/1.0/1.0 + 0.5 block-level.
  The verbatim reading is retained (high confidence); the other treated as an extraction misread.
- **Low confidence, flagged:** per-capita residential-area land for 15-min circles (22-28 m²/p from a
  single extraction of Table 4.0.1) — kept as indicative only.
